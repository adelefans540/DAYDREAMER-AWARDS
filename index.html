Daydreamers Awards

The Daydreamers Awards stand as the ultimate honor created by and for the global Adele fan community. Born from a profound admiration for her legacy, these awards pay tribute to the passion, creative brilliance, and unwavering commitment of our most outstanding members and content creators.

Daydreamers Awards Manifesto

The Origin of the Name

Inspired by "Daydreamer", the iconic opening track from Adele's debut album (19), these awards celebrate those who dreamt big and turned their admiration for the artist into a meeting point, art, and community.

The Purpose

To recognize the tireless work of content creators, fanpage administrators, editors, music analysts, and active members who keep Adele's cultural legacy alive across all digital platforms.

The Meaning of the Logo

The monumental "D" represents the unity of the fandom, while the vintage microphone at its center evokes the essence of singing and voice. The small integrated silhouettes honor every individual fan who supports this project.

Main Categories

Content Creator of the Year

Awarded to the channel or account that has produced the best visual, informative, or analytical content about the artist's career.

Hometown Glory Legend

Destined for the most active, supportive, and dedicated member within the community's platforms (TikTok, Instagram, fan groups, etc).

Chasing Pavements Creative Award

Directed at designers, video editors, and graphic artists who stand out for the aesthetic quality of their Adele-inspired creations.

The Value of the Award

More than just a distinction, winning a Daydreamer Award represents the highest status of respect within the community: a recognition that validates time, dedication, and love for Adele's music, immortalizing the winner's name in fandom history.
